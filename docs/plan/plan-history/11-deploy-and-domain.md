# 11 — Deploy & domain

Type: grilling
Status: resolved

## Question

Where does the site deploy, and on what domain?

## Answer

**Vercel + custom personal domain** (e.g. mitchmele.dev — exact name chosen at purchase
time). The site ships on the free `.vercel.app` URL first; buying and connecting the
domain is its own implementation issue (HITL — purchase requires the user).
